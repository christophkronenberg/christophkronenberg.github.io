Applied Econ Collective

Together with Hans Sievertsen and Yajna Govind, I run the Applied Econ Collective. The collective is funded entirely through donations and reimburses early-career applied economists for research costs between €10 and €200, for example journal submission fees, workshop attendance, software and datasets. It does not provide open-ended microgrants or stipends.

Applicants need an expense tied to a specific research project. The collective funds requests that meet three criteria: the cost is directly related to applied economics research, alternative funding is not readily available, and the applicant has not received funding from the collective in the last 12 months. Applicants from any country are eligible. Requests and donations go through the collective's Open Collective page, which also shows all donations and payouts and whether requests are currently open.
