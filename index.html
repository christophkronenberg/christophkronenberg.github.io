Christoph Kronenberg

Interim Professor of Public Economics, University of Duisburg-Essen

I am a public, health, and labor economist. Using causal inference methods and administrative data, I study the supply of mental health care, prescribing, and the effects of education reforms and occupational licensing. I founded and organize the Economics of Mental Health Workshop and convene the Essen Health Economics Seminar with Veda Ramakrishnan (RWI) and Kinshuk Taneja (RWI).
